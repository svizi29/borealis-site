What it does

Image

Prompt plus up to ten references; pick a look and a size and go.

Video

From text or from start and end frames, at the length and shape you need.

Inpaint

Paint a region, say what it becomes; the rest stays exactly as it was.

Upscale

Two or four times larger, careful with faces and text.

Canvas

An endless board of images, clips and prompts: group, connect, generate from a selection.

✨ Enhance

Write it in your own words; Enhance turns them into the full prompt.

One library, on your own drive

Northlight, Iceberg and Borealis share one library in your own Google Drive. Previews stream to every device, originals stay backed up, edits travel as small files. A rating you give in one app is there in the others.

Free, or Pro

Free

Pro
